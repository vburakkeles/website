Turkeyana Redesign

A full redesign to earn the trust of an international audience.

ResearchWireframingUX/UI DesignPrototypingResponsive Web
Product overview

Turkeyana Clinic serves patients from around the world. Its site looked dated and didn't turn international visitors into booked consultations. The redesign rebuilt the visual identity, the operation pages and the consultation funnel as a responsive, multilingual-ready system.

Problem statement

The old site undersold the clinic's quality and made the consultation request hard to find, especially for non-Turkish-speaking visitors.

For a medical decision made from another country, trust is the entire interface.

Design features
Feature 01

Operations, explained

Every procedure gets a structured page — what it is, stages, before/after and concerns — instead of a paragraph and a phone number.

Feature 02

Graft calculator

A simple calculator on hair-transplant pages gives visitors a concrete estimate and a reason to start a consultation.

Feature 03

Guided consultation

A four-step request — concern, details, photos, contact — replaces a long form and works on a phone.

Feature 04

Editorial trust

Blogs, doctor profiles and results give the site the credibility of a medical institution rather than a marketing page.

Design implications

The mobile share, interest in before-and-after content and consultation-form abandonment informed a mobile-first layout and clearer procedure pages. These descriptive figures do not establish a causal increase in consultation requests.

Key takeaways
01

Credibility beats urgency

The redesign prioritizes credible results, clear procedure information and accessible consultation entry points. Their effect on qualified inquiries requires post-launch measurement.

02

Structure is trust

Turning procedures into structured pages with stages and FAQs answered the questions visitors had been asking on WhatsApp.

03

Design for the first device

Building mobile-first matched how international patients actually arrive, and simplified every decision after.
