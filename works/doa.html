Product overview

DOA (Depozitosu Olan Ambalajlar) is Turkey's nationwide recycling platform that lets citizens return beverage containers and get their deposit back. It integrates QR scanning, return-machine (RVM) locations, e-wallet tracking, and a reward system — making recycling effortless for everyone.

Problem statement

Recycling rates in Turkey are low due to limited awareness, complex processes, and few incentives. People often throw recyclable containers away because returning them isn't convenient or rewarding.

People recycle when it's easier to return a bottle than to throw it away.

Design features
Feature 01

Balance you can see

The home screen leads with the deposit balance and a monthly recycling summary — the reward is visible before anything else.

Feature 02

Machines on a map

Nearby return points with distance and machine count, so a trip can be planned instead of guessed.

Feature 03

Refund your way

Deposits can stay in the e-wallet, be spent, or be transferred to a bank account in a couple of taps.

Feature 04

Impact that motivates

Total items, CO₂ saved and energy saved turn a chore into a visible contribution.

Design implications

The survey pointed to three priorities: explain deposit returns, help people locate machines, and make refunds clear. Full-word labels were selected to support a broad age range; age-group completion rates are not established by the figures shown here.

High fidelity screens
Screen 1

Home

Current balance plus a monthly recycling summary by material type. Quick actions (Pay, Receive, Transfer) and a recent-transactions list build transparency and trust.

Screen 2

Return points

An interactive map with clear markers shows nearby machines and distances. A detail card gives exact location and machine count, so trips are easy to plan.

Screen 3

Recycling history

Celebrates impact with a hero message — total recycled items, CO₂ reduction, energy saved — plus per-material stats and a most-used-machines list to reinforce the habit.

Key takeaways
01

Clarity is the reward

Age-friendly defaults and a transparent balance screen address the need to understand the return process and refund status. Their effect on adoption requires follow-up measurement.

02

Words over icons

Full-word labels on critical actions make the intended action explicit across the interface. Accessibility should be evaluated with participants across age and ability groups.

03

Make the impact visible

Monthly summaries and environmental-impact estimates were designed to encourage repeat returns. Habit formation and the assumptions behind impact estimates require separate validation.
