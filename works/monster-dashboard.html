Monster Dashboard

Finding the signal in a tool that was all noise.

ResearchWireframingUX/UI DesignPrototypingDashboardAdmin Panels
Product overview

Monster Dashboard is a comprehensive operations panel for a gaming-hardware brand. It centralizes financial metrics, product and inventory management, blog content, campaigns and customer support into one streamlined interface — so marketing analysts and managers can make data-driven decisions without switching tools.

Problem statement

Analysts and managers juggle fragmented reporting systems and switch between tools, which slows decisions and blocks a holistic view of business performance.

A dashboard's job is to answer questions — not to display everything it knows.
Goals
1

Centralize all financial and marketing data in one dashboard

2

Provide real-time insights with intuitive visualizations

3

Reduce reporting time and improve decision-making

4

Give analysts drill-down access to detailed data

Design features
Feature 01

Answers on top

Four KPIs with sparklines and deltas open every session; charts and lists below explain them rather than compete with them.

Feature 02

Inventory that flags itself

The products view carries stock status and a 'needs attention' panel, so low stock is discovered, not searched for.

Feature 03

Content and campaigns inside

Blog posts and campaigns are created, previewed and measured in the same panel that shows their results.

Feature 04

Support with context

Tickets and live chat show the customer's order and product beside the conversation.

Key takeaways
01

Defaults beat customization

Designing strong defaults beat designing for endless customization — most people just want the right view to already be there.

02

Hierarchy comes from questions

The Overview prioritizes the recurring reporting questions in the stakeholder interviews.

03

Context is the feature

Linking support, products and campaigns to shared records was designed to reduce context switching; time savings require measurement.
